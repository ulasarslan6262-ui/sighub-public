Thesis

The useful product begins where information stops.

Operational problems rarely arrive as product ideas. They arrive as repeated manual work, information nobody acts on, unclear ownership, or a customer problem that keeps returning in another form.

I work on that last mile: turning observable friction into a clear decision, then building the system that makes the decision easier.
