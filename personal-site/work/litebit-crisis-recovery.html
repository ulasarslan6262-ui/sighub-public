Case study 03 · Leadership and recovery

LiteBit
crisis recovery

Leading a support operation through backlog, pressure and trust recovery.

A data breach created a support crisis with roughly 3,000 open tickets and a three-week delay. Customer trust had deteriorated, the team was under sustained pressure and normal support operations were no longer sufficient.

As Customer Support Team Lead, I was responsible for the team, daily operations and the recovery structure.

20+Agents led
~3,000Tickets cleared
2.4 to 4.0Trustpilot recovery

The challenge.

The immediate problem was volume. The deeper problem was maintaining consistent decisions, escalations and communication while pressure was highest.

The approach.

01 / TRIAGE

Create a recovery operating rhythm.

Structured the backlog and priorities so the team could work through the crisis without losing sight of the most urgent customer risks.

02 / LEADERSHIP

Keep a 20+ person team effective.

Owned recruitment, onboarding, coaching and day-to-day leadership while supporting the team through high-pressure conditions.

03 / SYSTEMS

Stabilize workflows and escalation.

Optimized Zendesk configuration, macros and structured escalation systems to improve response consistency and operational stability.

04 / TRUST

Rebuild the customer relationship.

Combined backlog recovery with clearer communication and more reliable service execution.

Result.

The roughly 3,000-ticket backlog was cleared, operations stabilized and Trustpilot improved from about 2.4 to 4.0. The experience became a lasting lesson in how volume exposes every unclear ownership decision in a support system.
