Case study 02 · Customer operations

Speaksee
customer operations

Connecting customer data, onboarding, support and product feedback.

Speaksee builds hardware and software for accessibility. How customers get started and what support they need varies by product.

I lead customer experience across support, product and operations and report directly to the COO.

CX strategyHubSpotMixpanelJourney designAI onboardingSelf-service

The challenge.

Support and product teams needed a shared view of where customers got stuck, what they had already tried and who should follow up.

The approach.

01 / DATA

Establish a shared customer source.

Set up HubSpot as the shared source of customer data for support, sales and operations.

02 / INSIGHT

Make journey behaviour measurable.

Built Mixpanel dashboards to see where customers stopped progressing across product segments.

03 / RETENTION

Translate signals into workflows.

Designed customer journey maps, health scoring models and churn-reduction workflows across multiple product segments.

04 / SCALE

Increase capacity without adding headcount.

Expanded self-service with FAQs and chatbot flows and built an AI onboarding platform for employees, buddies and administrators.

Result.

The team expanded support capacity without adding headcount. Support, sales and operations could see the same customer history, and product had a clearer view of where customers needed help.
